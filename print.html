1-A APPLICABLE REGULATIONS

Statement of compliance with Regulation (EC) N.1907/2006 (REACH)

With specific reference to Regulation (EC) N.1907/2006 and further amendments (REACH), art. 3(3), cosmetic packagings produced by Vetronaviglio S.r.l. are considered “articles”. Therefore, with specific reference to REACH art. 33, we hereby declare that none of the products listed in the chart above contains any substance included in REACH Annex XIV, as defined by REACH art. 57, or included in the “Candidate list” published by ECHA (Substances of Very High Concern - SVHC), in concentration exceeding 0.1% (weight/weight), as defined by REACH art. 59 (1), and in conformity to REACH art. 59 (10). Please note that, in accordance to both the Regulation (EC) N.1907/2006 (REACH) and the Regulation (EC) N.1272/2008 (CLP), Vetronaviglio S.r.l. is not a supplier of substances nor preparations, and is therefore not required to send MSDSs to their customers.

Statement of compliance with European Parliament and Council Directive N.94/62/EC

We hereby declare that our products meet the requirements stated by European Directive 94/62/EC “on packaging and packaging waste”, including further amendments. In particular, the levels of heavy metals in our products are within the range of 100 ppm (w/w), in compliance to Dir. 94/62/EC art. 11.

Statement of compliance according to CE Cosmetics Regulation N.1223/2009

Vetronaviglio S.r.l. confirms that the above articles and/or components do not contain substances listed in Annex II (banned substances) and Annex III (under restriction substances) of CE Cosmetics Regulation N. 1223/2009. The materials used do not contain significant impurities, and, to our knowledge, our products do not present phenomena of migration of chemicals when they come into contact with their contents. However, we recommend our customers to perform the tests which apply to their formulations.

Statement of compliance to GMPs (Regulation N.2023/2006/EC)

Vetronaviglio S.r.l. confirms that the above articles and/or components are manufactured in compliance with good manufacturing practices (GMP). Such GMPs are those described by the CE Regulation N.2023/2006 for food packaging, which comprises a quality assurance and control system, dedicated process design and equipment, documentation and appropriate qualification of staff. Vetronaviglio S.r.l. applies these GMPs to all stages of manufacturing, processing and distribution of its products.
